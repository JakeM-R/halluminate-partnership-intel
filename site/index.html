Method and limits

Source

OpenAlex, queried daily by a GitHub Action. A paper counts toward a benchmark if it cites the benchmark's paper or names the benchmark in its title or abstract. For names that collide with other fields (τ-bench, AppWorld, GAIA, which is also a star survey), the abstract must also mention agents or language models.

Baseline

For each organisation: papers in the last 90 days against the 12 months before that. Rising means at least two recent papers, at least 1.5 times its usual rate, and a Poisson-style score (recent − expected) ÷ √(expected + 1) of 1 or more. A first-time organisation with two papers counts as rising, flagged "new".

Organisation types

From OpenAlex institution types, with a manual override file for frontier labs and large enterprises (overrides.json). Subsidiaries are merged (Microsoft Research Asia into Microsoft, for example).

Limits

  • OpenAlex doesn't yet index Halluminate's own benchmarks, so the graph is built on their neighbours.
  • About a third of recent arXiv papers have no resolved institutions in OpenAlex yet; they count toward benchmark totals but not toward any organisation.
  • Papers are a lagging and academic-leaning signal. Labs that build on benchmarks privately don't show up.
  • Organisations only. No individual is named; author lists are on the linked papers.
