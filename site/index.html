What you'd be doing

Halluminate builds training environments and benchmarks for AI agents doing finance work. Experts write realistic tasks, the kind an analyst does on a live deal, and review how models answer them. The aim is to find exactly where a model's reasoning breaks so it can be trained to do better.

The review is the core skill. A good reviewer:

  1. Checks every figure against the source, not against what looks plausible.
  2. Names the kind of error, not just that something is off. "Wrong add-back" is useful to a trainer; "this seems wrong" isn't.
  3. Follows an early mistake through to every number it moves, and to the conclusion.
  4. Leaves correct work alone. Flagging sound claims costs everyone time.

Next you'll review one model answer to a diligence question. Mark each claim as sound or wrong, then say whether you'd sign it off. Feedback is instant.
