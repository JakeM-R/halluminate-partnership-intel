Columns the export needs

Export invoices from HubSpot or QuickBooks as CSV, one row per invoice, including paid ones if you want the late-history modifier. Header names don't need to match exactly: common names are recognised and you can match anything else by hand.

Slash dates are read as US (MM/DD/YYYY). ISO dates (2026-10-16) and "Oct 16, 2026" also work.

Aging by customer

Open balances by days past due at the run date, sorted by dollars at risk (older money weighs more).

Chaser drafts

One email per customer, covering every open invoice. The tier comes from the oldest overdue invoice; tone moves a step with payment history. Tap a customer to open the draft, edit it in place, then copy or open in Gmail.

Make it run itself

    
A Google Apps Script with the same aging, rubric and templates as this page (it's the same code). Every 14 days it reads the invoice export in a Sheet, saves one Gmail draft per customer for you to review, and posts the digest to Slack. Nothing sends without you.

    
    
      
  1. Paste the HubSpot or QuickBooks invoice export into a Google Sheet, in a tab named Invoices, headers in row 1.
    2. 
      
  2. Open Extensions › Apps Script, replace the contents of Code.gs with the script below, and save.
    3. 
      
  3. In Project Settings › Script properties, add SENDER_NAME, SENDER_ROLE, FOUNDER_NAME, FOUNDER_ROLE and a Slack incoming webhook as SLACK_WEBHOOK_URL.
    4. 
      
  4. Run setup once and grant access to Sheets, Gmail and external requests. It adds a trigger for every other Monday at 8am.
    5. 
      
  5. Run run once to check: drafts land in Gmail, the digest posts to Slack, and an AR runs tab logs the totals.
    6.

Prototype by Jake Makking-Robinson for SnapMagic's finance operations remit ("run aging reports every two weeks"). Demo customers are generic (A to L) and demo amounts show as $xx; upload your own export to see real figures.

    
Parsing and aging run in your browser. When Claude drafting is on, only a per-customer summary (name, balances, days overdue, invoice list, late history) is sent; no emails or raw rows. Nothing is stored. Drafts that break a rule fall back to fixed templates.
